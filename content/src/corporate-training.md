# Brightpath Training — Corporate and Group Training
## Private team training
Any Brightpath course can be delivered privately for your team, online or at your offices anywhere in the continental United States. Private courses can be customised with your company's own case studies and tools.

## Pricing for private courses
Private delivery is priced per day rather than per student: $4,800 per day online and $5,900 per day on-site, for up to 18 participants. Instructor travel is included for on-site delivery within Texas; outside Texas, travel is billed at cost.

## Training credits
Organisations can prepay for training credits. One credit equals $100 of training value. Purchases of 200 credits or more receive 10 percent bonus credits. Credits are valid for 18 months.

## Account management
Corporate clients with an annual spend over $25,000 receive a dedicated account manager, quarterly training reports and priority access to new courses.

## Getting a quote
To request a team quote, email corporate@brightpathtraining.example with your team size, preferred course, format (online or on-site) and target dates. Quotes are sent within 2 business days.
