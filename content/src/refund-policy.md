# Brightpath Training — Refund, Cancellation and Transfer Policy
## Cancellations by students
- Cancel 14 or more calendar days before the course start date: full refund, minus a $50 administration fee.
- Cancel 7 to 13 calendar days before the start date: 50 percent refund, or a free transfer to another date.
- Cancel less than 7 days before the start date, or fail to attend: no refund, but you may transfer to a future date once for a $150 transfer fee.

## Transfers and substitutions
You may transfer to another date of the same course free of charge up to 14 days before the start date. A colleague may take your place at any time at no extra charge; just email the new student's name to enroll@brightpathtraining.example.

## Cancellations by Brightpath
If Brightpath cancels or reschedules a course, you may choose a full refund or a free transfer to another date. Brightpath is not responsible for travel or hotel costs.

## Exam vouchers
Exam vouchers that have already been issued (for example the PSM I voucher) are non-refundable.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 10 business days of the cancellation request.

## Pass guarantee
The PMP Exam Prep Bootcamp pass guarantee allows one free retake of the bootcamp if you fail the PMP exam within 90 days of completing the course. You must send a copy of your exam score report.
