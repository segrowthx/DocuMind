## Discounts
- Early bird discount: 10 percent off when you enroll and pay at least 30 days before the course start date.
- Group discount: 15 percent off per student when 3 to 9 people from the same organisation enroll in the same course. Groups of 10 or more should request a private team quote (see Corporate and Group Training).
- Alumni discount: 20 percent off any course for past Brightpath students who completed a course in the last 24 months.
- Military veterans receive 10 percent off.

Only one discount can be applied per enrollment; discounts cannot be combined.

## Bundles
- Data Analyst Path (DA-110 + DA-120 + DA-210): $3,400, saving $495 compared to individual prices.
- Project Leader Path (PM-101 + LD-100): $2,900.

## Payment options
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, ACH bank transfer and company purchase orders. Interest-free payment plans are available for courses over $1,000: pay in 3 equal monthly instalments, with the first instalment due at enrollment.
