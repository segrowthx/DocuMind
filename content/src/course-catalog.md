# Brightpath Training — Course Catalog 2027
Brightpath Training is a professional skills training company based in Austin, Texas, founded in 2015. We deliver instructor-led courses in project management, data analytics, digital marketing and leadership, both live online and at our Austin training center (410 Congress Avenue, Suite 900).

## How our courses work
Every course includes live instructor sessions, hands-on labs, downloadable workbooks, and a certificate of completion. Students who attend at least 80 percent of live sessions and pass the final assessment with a score of 70 percent or higher receive a Brightpath Certificate. Class sizes are capped at 18 students.

## Project Management
### PMP Exam Prep Bootcamp (Course code PM-101)
A 5-day intensive (35 contact hours) that satisfies the 35 hours of project management education required by PMI to sit the PMP exam. Covers predictive, agile and hybrid approaches, plus 4 full-length practice exams. Prerequisite: at least 3 years of project experience. Includes a pass guarantee: students who fail the PMP exam within 90 days of the course may retake the bootcamp free of charge.

### Agile Scrum Master Certification (Course code PM-201)
A 2-day course (16 hours) preparing students for the Professional Scrum Master I (PSM I) exam. No prerequisites. Exam voucher included in the course fee.

### Microsoft Project Essentials (Course code PM-150)
A 1-day course (7 hours) on scheduling, resource management and reporting in Microsoft Project. Students need access to Microsoft Project; a 30-day trial licence is provided.

## Data Analytics
### Data Analytics with Excel and Power BI (Course code DA-110)
A 4-week part-time course, two evenings per week (6:00 pm to 9:00 pm Central Time), 24 hours total. Covers pivot tables, Power Query, DAX basics and dashboard design. Prerequisite: basic Excel skills.

### Python for Data Analysis (Course code DA-210)
A 6-week part-time course, Saturdays 9:00 am to 1:00 pm Central Time, 24 hours total. Covers pandas, data cleaning, visualisation with matplotlib and an introduction to machine learning with scikit-learn. No prior programming experience is required. Students need a laptop that can run Python 3.12.

### SQL Fundamentals (Course code DA-120)
A 2-day course (14 hours) covering SELECT queries, joins, aggregation, window functions and query performance basics using PostgreSQL.

## Digital Marketing
### Digital Marketing Professional (Course code DM-100)
An 8-week part-time course covering SEO, paid search, social media advertising, email marketing and analytics. Includes a capstone project in which students build and present a full campaign plan.

### Google Ads Masterclass (Course code DM-220)
A 1-day course (7 hours) focused on search campaign structure, bidding strategies and conversion tracking.

## Leadership
### New Manager Essentials (Course code LD-100)
A 3-day course (21 hours) for people in their first two years of managing others. Covers delegation, feedback conversations, one-to-ones and performance management.

### Leading Through Change (Course code LD-300)
A 2-day course (14 hours) for senior managers on planning and communicating organisational change.
