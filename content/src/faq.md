# Brightpath Training — Frequently Asked Questions
## Are online classes live or recorded?
All online classes are live, instructor-led sessions on Zoom. Recordings of each session are available in the Brightpath Learning Portal for 60 days after the course ends.

## Will I get a certificate?
Yes. Students who attend at least 80 percent of live sessions and score 70 percent or higher on the final assessment receive a digital Brightpath Certificate within 5 business days.

## Do your courses count toward PMI PDUs?
Yes. Brightpath is a PMI Authorized Training Partner. Project management and leadership courses earn 1 PDU per contact hour.

## What equipment do I need for online classes?
A computer with a webcam, microphone, a stable internet connection of at least 10 Mbps and the Zoom desktop app. Data analytics courses also require a laptop where you can install software.

## Is parking available at the Austin center?
Validated parking is available in the 4th Street garage for $8 per day. The center is also a 5-minute walk from the Congress Avenue bus stops.

## Is lunch provided at in-person classes?
Yes. Full-day classes at the Austin training center include lunch, coffee and snacks. Please tell us about dietary requirements when you enroll.

## Can I get a refund if I change my mind?
Yes, depending on how much notice you give. See the Refund, Cancellation and Transfer Policy for details.

## Do you offer financing?
Courses over $1,000 can be paid in 3 interest-free monthly instalments.
