# Brightpath Training — How to Enroll
## Step 1: Choose your course and date
Browse the course catalog and public schedule, then pick a date. If you are unsure which course fits, book a free 15-minute advising call with an enrollment advisor.

## Step 2: Complete the enrollment form
Enroll online at brightpathtraining.example/enroll or by calling (512) 555-0142, Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 6:00 pm Central Time. You will need the student's full name, email address, phone number and billing details.

## Step 3: Pay or submit a purchase order
Payment is due at enrollment unless you choose a payment plan. Companies paying by purchase order must submit the PO at least 10 business days before the course starts.

## Step 4: Receive your welcome pack
Within 1 business day of enrollment you will receive a confirmation email with your welcome pack: joining instructions, the pre-course reading list and login details for the Brightpath Learning Portal. Physical workbooks for Austin classes are handed out on the first morning.

## Enrollment deadlines
Enrollment closes 3 business days before a course starts, or earlier if the class is full. Waitlist places are offered in the order requests are received.

## Accessibility
Please tell us about any accessibility needs when you enroll. We provide captioning for all online sessions on request, and the Austin training center is wheelchair accessible.
